Northwind Logistics - Travel and Accommodation Policy

Booking
All travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal at least 14
days before departure where practicable. Bookings made inside 14 days require
written approval from a department head. The cheapest reasonable fare is
expected; a more expensive option must be justified in the booking notes.

Air travel
Economy class is standard for all flights under six hours. Premium economy is
permitted for flights over six hours, and business class only for flights
over ten hours or where a member of staff has a documented medical need.
Seat selection fees up to 40 pounds per leg are reimbursable.

Accommodation
The nightly accommodation cap is 180 pounds in London, 140 pounds elsewhere
in the United Kingdom, and 160 pounds for international travel. The cap
includes taxes but excludes breakfast where breakfast is charged separately.
Staff may stay with friends or family instead and claim a flat 30 pounds per
night in lieu.

Subsistence
Meal allowances are 12 pounds for breakfast, 18 pounds for lunch and 30
pounds for dinner. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Receipts are required for any
single meal above 20 pounds.

Ground transport
Rail travel should be standard class unless the journey exceeds three hours.
Taxis are reimbursable where public transport is impractical or where the
journey begins or ends between 22:00 and 06:00. Car hire requires prior
approval and must be compact class or smaller.
