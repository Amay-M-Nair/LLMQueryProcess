Northwind Logistics - New Starter Onboarding

Before the first day
The hiring manager must confirm the start date with people operations at
least 10 working days in advance so that equipment can be ordered. Right to
work documents must be verified before the first day, without exception.

First day
New starters attend a half-day induction covering the company structure,
information security, and health and safety. Laptops are issued at induction
and must be collected in person. Building access cards are activated by 09:00
on the start date.

Probation
The standard probation period is six months for permanent staff and three
months for fixed-term contracts under a year. A probation review is held at
the midpoint and at the end. Probation may be extended once, by up to three
months, with written reasons.

Training
All staff complete information security training within the first two weeks
and annually thereafter. Staff handling customer data also complete data
protection training within the first month. Training completion is tracked by
people operations and reported quarterly.

Buddy scheme
Every new starter is assigned a buddy from a different team for their first
three months. The buddy is not the line manager and has no assessment role.
