Northwind Logistics - Information Security Standard

Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are not required to be rotated
on a schedule, in line with current guidance. Multi-factor authentication is
mandatory for all remote access and for any account with administrative
privileges. Hardware security keys are issued to staff in engineering and
finance.

Device management
Company laptops are encrypted at rest using full-disk encryption. A device
must be reported lost or stolen within 24 hours. Personal devices may access
company email only through the managed application, never through a native
mail client.

Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted.
Confidential data may not be copied to removable media. Restricted data may
only be processed on company-managed devices within the corporate network or
over the approved VPN.

Incident reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@northwind-example.com
within one hour of discovery. The on-call security engineer responds within
30 minutes during business hours and within two hours outside them. Staff are
never penalised for reporting an incident that turns out to be benign.

Third parties
Vendors handling Confidential or Restricted data must complete a security
assessment before contract signature, and be reassessed annually.
