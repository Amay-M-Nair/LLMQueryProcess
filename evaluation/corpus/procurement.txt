Northwind Logistics - Procurement Standard

Thresholds
Purchases below 500 pounds may be approved by a line manager. Purchases
between 500 and 10,000 pounds require two written quotations and department
head approval. Purchases above 10,000 pounds require a competitive tender
with at least three bidders and sign-off from the finance director.

Preferred suppliers
Where a preferred supplier exists for a category, it must be used unless a
documented exception is approved. The preferred supplier list is reviewed
every six months by the procurement committee.

Contracts
No contract may be signed by anyone other than a director or a person holding
written delegated authority. Contracts with an initial term longer than three
years require board approval. Automatic renewal clauses must be flagged to
finance at the point of signature.

Conflicts of interest
Any personal relationship with a supplier or bidder must be declared in
writing before the procurement begins. Staff who have declared a conflict may
not participate in evaluation or scoring.

Payment terms
Standard payment terms are 30 days from receipt of a valid invoice. Early
settlement discounts should be taken where the discount exceeds one percent.
Disputed invoices must be raised with the supplier within 10 working days.
