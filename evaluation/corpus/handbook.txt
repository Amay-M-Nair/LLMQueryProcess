Northwind Logistics - Employee Handbook

Section 1: Working hours
Standard hours are 09:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, with an unpaid lunch
break of one hour. Core hours during which all staff must be contactable are
10:00 to 16:00. Flexible start times between 07:30 and 10:00 are permitted
with line manager approval. Overtime is paid at 1.5 times the standard rate
on weekdays and 2.0 times on Sundays and public holidays.

Section 2: Annual leave
Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in
addition to public holidays. Leave accrues monthly at 2.08 days per month.
Unused leave may be carried over to a maximum of five days, and carried-over
days expire on 31 March of the following year. Leave requests of five days or
more require four weeks notice.

Section 3: Sick leave
Sick leave is granted separately from annual leave and does not count against
it. Employees must notify their line manager before 10:00 on the first day of
absence. A self-certification form covers absences up to seven consecutive
days. A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required for
absences longer than seven consecutive days. Company sick pay covers the
first 20 working days of absence in any rolling twelve-month period.

Section 4: Parental leave
Parental leave follows statutory minimums with an enhancement: the first six
weeks are paid at full salary rather than the statutory rate. Employees must
give 15 weeks notice before the expected week of childbirth. Shared parental
leave is available and must be agreed with the line manager at least eight
weeks in advance.

Section 5: Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. A permanent remote
arrangement requires approval from a department head and a home workstation
assessment. The company contributes 200 pounds towards home office equipment
once every three years.

Section 6: Expenses
Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expenditure. Receipts
are required for any claim above 25 pounds. Mileage is reimbursed at 45 pence
per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year and 25 pence thereafter.
Claims are paid with the next payroll run following approval.
