Northwind Logistics - Service Incident Management

Severity levels
Severity 1 means a complete loss of service affecting all customers.
Severity 2 means a major feature is unavailable or a subset of customers is
fully blocked. Severity 3 means degraded performance with a workaround
available. Severity 4 covers cosmetic issues and minor defects.

Response targets
Severity 1 incidents require acknowledgement within 15 minutes and updates
every 30 minutes until resolved. Severity 2 requires acknowledgement within
one hour and updates every two hours. Severity 3 is acknowledged within one
business day. Severity 4 is handled in the normal backlog.

Roles
Every incident has a single incident commander, who coordinates but does not
debug. A separate communications lead handles customer updates for Severity 1
and 2. The on-call engineer may escalate to the incident commander at any
time without justification.

Postmortems
A written postmortem is required for every Severity 1 and 2 incident, within
five working days of resolution. Postmortems are blameless: they describe
what the system allowed to happen, not who typed what. Action items must have
a named owner and a due date.

Communication
Customer-facing status updates are published to the status page. Internal
updates go to the incident channel. No individual customer is told anything
that has not first appeared on the status page.
