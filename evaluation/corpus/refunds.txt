Northwind Logistics - Customer Refund Policy

Overview
This policy governs refunds for goods and services purchased directly from
Northwind Logistics. It does not apply to purchases made through third-party
resellers, which are governed by the reseller's own terms.

Standard refund window
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, provided the
item is returned in its original packaging and in resalable condition. The
window is extended to 60 days for purchases made in December.

Digital goods
Digital goods are non-refundable once downloaded, except where the file is
demonstrably corrupt or does not match its description. A download is
considered to have occurred when the file transfer completes.

Shipping charges
Shipping charges are not refunded unless the return results from an error on
our part, such as a mis-picked or damaged item. Return postage is paid by the
customer except in those circumstances.

Processing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically appear within
five business days of approval. Refunds to expired cards are issued as
account credit instead. Approval normally takes two business days from receipt
of the returned item.

Exceptions
Bespoke or personalised items cannot be refunded unless faulty. Bulk orders
above 5,000 pounds are subject to a 15 percent restocking fee.

Contact
For questions about a refund, contact returns@northwind-example.com or call
0800 555 0199 between 09:00 and 17:00 on weekdays. The returns coordinator is
Priya Raghunathan, reference desk QX-4471.
